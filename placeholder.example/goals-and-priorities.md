# Goals and Priorities

What the operator is currently optimizing for. Updated as priorities shift.

## What to include

- **Professional goals** — what they're trying to achieve at work this year / quarter
- **Project goals** — what they want their side projects or initiatives to reach
- **Personal goals** — anything that affects how they want to spend time

## Structure suggestion

Group by horizon:

```
## This quarter
- ...

## This year
- ...

## Longer-term
- ...
```

## Maintenance

Touch this file when:
- A goal is achieved (move to a "Completed" section or remove)
- Priorities shift (update the active list)
- A new project becomes the focus

It should not get stale — if the AI is making suggestions based on goals from
two quarters ago, the recommendations will feel off.

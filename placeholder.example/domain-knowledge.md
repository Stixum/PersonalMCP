# Domain Knowledge

Areas where the operator has deep expertise. Tells the AI what level of
explanation is appropriate — what to assume vs what to spell out.

## What to include

- Technical domains the operator knows deeply (e.g., specific languages, frameworks, cloud services)
- Functional domains (e.g., specific industries, regulatory environments, business functions)
- Methodologies and frameworks they apply (e.g., TDD, specific architectural patterns)
- Tools they're fluent in vs ones they're learning

## How the AI uses this

- Skip basic explanations in known domains
- Provide more scaffolding in adjacent / new domains
- Match vocabulary to expertise level
- Avoid over-explaining the obvious

## What NOT to include

- A resume (that's `role-and-responsibilities.md`)
- Specific projects (those are `current-projects.md`)

# Role and Responsibilities

What the operator actually does day-to-day. Where `identity.md` is the
"elevator pitch," this is "what's on their plate."

## What to include

- Job title and the scope it implies (team size, geographic reach, etc.)
- Primary deliverables — the artifacts they produce or own
- Industries / domains they focus on
- Decision authority — what they sign off on, what they advise on
- Recurring meeting cadences if relevant (e.g., "weekly portfolio review")

## What NOT to include

- Specific client names (operational risk; project-specific notes belong elsewhere)
- Current quarter goals (those go in `goals-and-priorities.md`)
- Personal interests

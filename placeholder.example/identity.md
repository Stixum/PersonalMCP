# Identity

The highest-level snapshot of who the operator is. Stable; rarely changes.

## What to include

- **Name** and what they go by
- **Title / primary role** (one line)
- **Employer / firm** (optional)
- **Location** (city, region — useful for time-zone and travel context)
- **One-paragraph summary** of what they do professionally — the "elevator pitch" an AI should be able to repeat back

## What NOT to include

- Detailed responsibilities (those go in `role-and-responsibilities.md`)
- Project lists (those go in `current-projects.md`)
- Goals (those go in `goals-and-priorities.md`)

## Example shape (replace with real content)

```
# Identity

## Professional
**Name:** <Full name>
**Title:** <Title, Company>
**Location:** <City>, <Region>

<One-paragraph summary of what they do, who they serve, what they're known for.>

## Personal
<Optional: where they're based, broad interests, anything that affects working context.>
```

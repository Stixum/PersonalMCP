# Preferences and Constraints

General work-style preferences that aren't strictly communication style and
aren't strictly operational rules. The "how I like to work" layer.

## What to include

- **Autonomy level** — when to act vs when to ask
- **Tool preferences** — editors, languages, frameworks, libraries the operator reaches for
- **Code style** — naming, structure, comments, test discipline
- **Decision style** — when to present options vs pick one, how much rationale to show
- **Working hours / context** — if relevant to async expectations

## Difference from `operational-rules.md`

- **This file:** preferences (soft, contextual, judgment calls)
- **operational-rules.md:** hard rules (deploy from clean source, never skip hooks, etc.)

When a preference becomes a hard rule (because violating it caused a real
incident), promote it to `operational-rules.md`.

## Difference from `communication-style.md`

- **This file:** how I like to work
- **communication-style.md:** how I like you to talk to me
